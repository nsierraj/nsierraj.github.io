## I build the platforms other people's work runs on.

Most companies keep two platforms apart: the service platform employees depend on, and the delivery platform engineers build on. I've run both, and connecting them is where the leverage is.

- **From one seat to a 14-person platform team.** Started as a company's only ServiceNow resource. Built the organization behind ITSM, CMDB, asset management and GRC for 10,000+ employees.
- **From ad hoc releases to 25–50 deploys a week.** Founded a DevOps practice from zero. Golden paths provision infrastructure, create a governed repo and wire up CI/CD in one step. Environment setup went from two weeks to hours.
- **AI that compounds instead of leaks.** Scaled AI coding assistants to 300+ developers at 64% active usage and put AI triage into production ITSM, with guardrails built into the workflows people already use.

25 years. One through-line: **self-service under governance.**
