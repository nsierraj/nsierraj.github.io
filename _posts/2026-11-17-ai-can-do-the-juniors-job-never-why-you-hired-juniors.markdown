Large companies are slowing graduate hiring on the bet that seniors with AI can
do the work. They may be right about the output, but they're wrong about what
juniors were for. On LinkedIn, I argue for keeping the pipeline open and not
handing new engineers the same AI setup as your seniors: one repo, two setups.
A shared CLAUDE.md for everyone, a learner layer for new engineers (Claude
Code's Learning style leaves TODO(human) markers for them to write), PR
explanations in their own words, and juniors on the review rotation. Read the
full article
[on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-can-do-juniors-job-never-why-you-hired-amado-sierra-mejwc/).
