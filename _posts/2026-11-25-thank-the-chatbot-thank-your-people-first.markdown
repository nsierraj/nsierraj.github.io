Most American office workers say "please" and "thank you" to AI, but the people
using it aren't getting much back. In a Thanksgiving-week post on LinkedIn, I
pulled numbers from a piece by David Rice at People Managing People: only 29%
say they get enough AI training, 31% of AI users say their employer gave them
more work because of it, and 77% of senior leaders find AI makes their work more
satisfying against 40% of entry-level workers. Every team now has AI leftovers,
the time it saves, and the question is who gets them. Read the full post
